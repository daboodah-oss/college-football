# Spec: Playoff Side by Side redesign — two lists, connector lines, rationale pop-ups

Repo: daboodah-oss/college-football. Build on your own branch, do not merge.
Design approved by Jeff 2026-11-24 after nine mockup rounds with Budzilla; this spec
pins the final mockup (v9). Display only: no changes to collect/, update.py,
cfp_model.py, or anything in data/. Frozen snapshots are read, never altered.

## What changes

The Playoff tab's "Side by side" section (template.html, `#cfp-side`, rendered by
`updateCfp()`) stops being a table and becomes two ranked lists with connector
lines, one list per ranking, plus a delta column and a per-team rationale pop-up.

### Layout (per selected week)

- Left list headed "CFP": the committee's 25 in committee order, each entry one
  container reading `1 - Georgia (9-1)` (rank, name, record), followed at the
  bottom by any teams that are in our Top 25 only, shown as `NR - LSU (6-4)`,
  ordered among themselves by our rank.
- Right list headed "Ours": our 25 in our order, followed at the bottom by any
  committee-only teams as NR entries, ordered among themselves by committee rank.
- Between the lists, an SVG connector joins each team's two entries with a
  smooth curve. Line color, by the week's two ranks (a team missing from a
  list counts as rank 26 for color only):
  - red when CFP rank < our rank (the committee ranks the team higher than we do)
  - green when CFP rank > our rank (the committee ranks the team lower)
  - blue when the ranks are equal
  Use the page's own variables: red = `--bad`, green = `--good`, blue = `--s1`,
  so both themes come out right. Lines are ~1.8px at ~55% opacity; hovering or
  tapping a team thickens its line to full opacity and dims the rest.
- A slim Δ column rides with the Ours list, one cell per Ours entry:
  Δ = CFP rank − our rank, shown signed, colored like its line (positive
  green, negative red, 0 blue), and `—` when either rank is NR.
- No sorting controls. Both lists have exactly one order (see above).
- The section eyebrow becomes "Two Top 25s, drawn against each other" and
  `#cfp-side` loses its `tscroll` class (it is no longer a table).

### Conference pill

Each entry carries a small pill at its right edge with the team's conference
abbreviation for the snapshot's season: Big Ten B1G, SEC SEC, Big 12 B12,
ACC ACC, Pac-12 P12, AAC AAC, Sun Belt SBC, MWC MWC, MAC MAC, CUSA CUSA,
Independent IND. Colors (light-theme text / dark-theme text, tinted
background of the same hue, ~12% alpha, border ~50%):
SEC gold #96690a / #eec95c; B1G blue #1d4ed8 / #7db4ff; B12 red #b42318 / #ff9a8a;
ACC green #14713c / #6fd598; P12 purple #6d28d9 / #c4b5fd; AAC teal #0f6a72 / #5fd4c8;
SBC orange #b45508 / #ffb37d; MWC pink #a02c5e / #f09ec0; MAC olive #4f6d00 / #b8d94e;
CUSA brown #7c5222 / #d9a05b; IND uses the page's muted/surface-2 neutrals.
Dark overrides must follow the page's existing pattern (both the
prefers-color-scheme block and `:root[data-theme="dark"]`). No conference
logos or external images — colors only, deliberately.

### Data: build_cfp.py

The page model needs each union team's conference. In `build_cfp.build`, load
the repo-root `conferences.json` `members` map for the snapshot's season (the
same source build_bias.py already receives via build_report.py) and add a
per-week `"conf": {team_id: conference_name}` covering the same union the
`names` map covers. `check()` that every union team has a membership entry for
the season. Nothing else about the model changes; all rationale fields the
pop-up needs (record, srs, sos_rank, quality_wins, top25_wins, best_win,
worst_loss, and wab/wab_rank on WAB weeks) are already in each week's `lines`.

### Rationale pop-up

Clicking or tapping any entry (either list) opens a pop-up card for that team,
positioned under the tapped entry and clamped inside the section. It closes on:
the × button, tapping the same team again, or a pointerdown anywhere outside
`#cfp-side` (one document-level listener, registered once — follow the page's
existing sticky-tooltip dismissal convention). Tapping a different team swaps
the content. Content is generated by rule from the week's stored line — no
hand-written text, no new data:

- Header: `Georgia (9-1)` plus the conference pill.
- Sub-line: `CFP #1 · Ours #1 · Δ 0`; for a one-list team, `in one Top 25 only`
  in place of the Δ.
- "What our model sees": SRS value and its rank among the week's union lines
  (computed in the page from the stored lines, ordinal via the existing `ord()`);
  `Schedule strength` ordinal; quality wins and Top-25 wins counts; best win
  (name + record) when present; on WAB weeks, when the line carries them,
  `Wins Above Bubble: No. <wab_rank> (<signed wab value, 2dp>)`.
- "What the committee sees": record (with `— unbeaten` when losses are 0);
  worst loss (name + record) or `No losses to weigh against them`; a
  `No Top-25 wins on the résumé` line when top25_wins is 0; a
  `No quality wins on the résumé` line when quality_wins is 0.
- Closing sentence, by direction (NR counts as 26):
  - Δ > 0: the model is pricing the efficiency and the schedule (SRS rank and
    schedule rank named); the committee is pricing the losses, the worst loss
    named. Same facts, different weights.
  - Δ < 0: the committee is pricing the record and the best win; the model is
    pricing the SRS rank and the schedule rank (plus `, with no Top-25 wins`
    when that holds).
  - Δ = 0: no gap to explain — both land in the same spot.
- Footer: `Generated from this snapshot's stored numbers.`

### WAB adaptations (Jeff has seen these flagged; they are part of this build)

1. The old table's WAB and WAB-value columns go away. A union team's WAB rank
   and value move into its rationale pop-up (above). The WAB agreement
   scorecard block above the section is unchanged.
2. A team in WAB's Top 25 but in neither list no longer gets a row (it has no
   entry in either list to connect). When such teams exist for the selected
   week, the caption names them: `In Wins Above Bubble's Top 25 only: <names>.`

### Caption

Rewrite the `#cfp-side-cap` text (updateCfp sets it, as today) to cover, in
plain words: Δ rides with our list and green means we rank the team higher;
the line colors (red = committee higher, green = committee lower, blue =
agreement); the pill is the team's conference this season; NR marks a team
missing from that list's Top 25; tap any team for the numbers behind its gap;
plus the WAB sentences from the adaptation above (pop-up pointer on WAB weeks,
the WAB-only naming when it applies, `Wins Above Bubble tracking began
<first WAB week>` on pre-WAB weeks, as today).

### Drawing mechanics

- Lines are SVG paths measured from the rendered rows' rectangles relative to
  the section body (the mockup approach). Each path carries its team id so a
  redraw only recomputes geometry. Redraw after: a week render, the Playoff
  tab opening (hook the existing `showTab`), and window resize. Register the
  resize/outside-dismiss listeners once, not per render.
- In jsdom the rectangles are all zero: the paths must still be emitted (the
  render checker counts them) and nothing may throw.
- Mind the repo's TDZ lesson: helpers used by `updateCfp()` must be defined
  before first execution; prefer function declarations for the new helpers.

## Amendment (2026-11-25, Jeff approved): WAB is a list, not just a column

This section supersedes "WAB adaptations" item 1 above (item 2 stands: WAB-only
teams are named in the caption, never rows). Jeff asked for WAB to be sortable,
then for all three lists at once on desktop. The section therefore ships in two
presentations of the same data, chosen by the section's own width:

### Narrow presentation (below ~920px container width): the swap view

- The layout in the main spec, plus the slim third column from the start: it
  shows each team's rank in the *other* numbers ranking, riding with the Ours
  list exactly like Δ does, headed by a swap button labeled with that other
  ranking's name ("WAB", with a "tap to swap" cue).
- Tapping the swap button makes the right list the WAB list: entries re-order
  by WAB rank and are labeled with WAB ranks, the right header reads "WAB",
  Δ becomes CFP − WAB, the connector lines re-color by CFP vs WAB, and the
  slim column flips to showing our ranks (headed "Ours", also a swap button).
  Tapping again swaps back. Default is Ours.
- The pop-up follows the view: sub-line shows all three ranks and the Δ
  against the ranking on the right; in WAB view the first evidence block is
  "What WAB sees" (WAB rank and signed value with a one-line gloss — wins
  earned above a bubble team's expected total on this schedule — then quality
  wins, Top-25 wins, best win, and where our model has them); the closing
  sentence is generated against the WAB gap (WAB pricing the wins themselves
  vs the committee pricing the losses, and mirror-image).
- Weeks without WAB have no slim column and no swap: exactly the main spec.

### Wide presentation (~920px and up): all three lists

- Five columns of content: CFP list, connector gutter, Ours list, Δ
  (CFP − Ours), connector gutter, WAB list, Δ (CFP − WAB). Each list carries
  the same combined entries and conference pills; each list's bottom carries
  its NR entries (teams missing from that list, ordered by committee rank).
  The union is still committee ∪ ours; WAB-only teams stay caption-named.
- Each team's trace is two segments (CFP→Ours, Ours→WAB), colored per
  segment by the same rule, generalised: red where the list on the left
  ranks the team higher than the list on the right, green where it ranks
  them lower, blue where equal; NR counts as 26 for color. Hover/tap
  highlights the whole trace.
- Pop-ups are the main spec's (the "What our model sees" form), with the WAB
  line in that block also carrying its Δ vs the committee.
- Weeks without WAB render the main spec's two lists at any width.

### Mechanics and checking

- Pick the presentation from the section's measured width on render and on
  resize (debounced is fine); when the width measures 0 (jsdom), use the
  narrow presentation so the checker exercises the swap. The wide
  presentation gets its first real exercise on the live page; say so in the
  handoff, and Budzilla verifies it there after the merge.
- Checker additions for a WAB week: the slim column's cells count = union
  size; clicking the swap button re-orders the right list so its first entry
  is the WAB No. 1 team and the right header reads "WAB"; clicking again
  restores our order. (The Week 13 snapshot is the first WAB week the checker
  will meet; Weeks 11/12 exercise the no-WAB path until the retrospective
  backfill lands.)
